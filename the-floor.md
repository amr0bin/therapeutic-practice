# The Floor

*Working note, 20 September 2026. The companion to `the-ceiling.md`. What survives the hardest year. Written now, in a season that is not hard, because a floor decided during a hard year is not a floor — it is whatever was left.*

---

## Why this file exists

This practice runs through two degrees, a job change and a practicum. A plan that assumes clear years is a plan that stops in 2027 and gets restarted, badly, in 2031 — with four years of record missing and nothing to show a panel.

Two stretches are already visible:

- **September 2027 – August 2029.** Leeds part-time, alongside a full-time role.
- **September 2033 – April 2034.** The practicum: 2.5–3 days a week on placement.

Both are known about seven and six years in advance respectively. That is the whole advantage, and it is wasted unless the collapse is *chosen* now rather than discovered then.

---

## The floor, in all seasons

Four things. Nothing else.

| | What | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One notebook entry a month** | Not weekly. The weekly cadence in `therapeutic-studio-the-approach.md` is a full-intensity figure and it will break during Leeds — and a floor that breaks is not a floor. Monthly still produces 48 dated entries before the 2030 panel reads anything. |
| 2 | **The hours log updated within 24 hours of any service** | The only record that cannot be reconstructed afterwards. Everything else in this system can be done late. |
| 3 | **The crisis-line shift kept** | It is the three-year clock UBC requires, and it is the first external supervisor this practice has. |
| 4 | **One calendar check a month** | Five minutes against `calendar.html`. Deadlines here are annual and unforgiving, and missing one costs a year. |

That is the entire floor. **Everything else is full-intensity work and is permitted to stop:** the review, the faculty map, the spoken piece, the resource documentation, the practitioner interviews, the boundary-page reviews.

---

## The floor during the practicum year, 2033–34

Smaller still. **The hours log, and nothing else.**

The placement *is* the practice that year: 600 hours, 180 of them direct, two hours of supervision a week. There is no version of that year in which a notebook entry is also produced, and pretending otherwise is how people arrive at registration exhausted and stop.

---

## What "at the floor" means, said plainly

Running at the floor is **success**, not neglect.

`00-portfolio-spine-shared.md` already establishes this for the portfolio: only one property runs at full intensity at a time. The counselling-specific version is that **a degree is not a pause in this practice — it is this practice at full intensity.** The MSc is Labs. The MA is Therapeutic Practice. Framed that way nothing is being abandoned in those years, which removes the particular guilt that otherwise attaches to them and, more practically, removes the temptation to overreach and lose the record entirely.

---

## The two rules underneath

**Accumulation, not intensity.** A year in which all five ledgers gained one small entry is a year on the trajectory. A heroic month that produced nothing recorded is not.

**Nothing free that should be paid.** Inherited from Studio Day and it holds here too, with a delay: nothing can be charged for before registration, so the pre-licence form of this rule is that training, prerequisites and conference travel should be *funded* — by a waiver, a bursary, an employer or a stipend — rather than absorbed. See the Funded ledger in `the-ledgers.md`.

---

## The failure this file is written against

Not dramatic. It is the year in which the Leeds workload rises, the weekly notebook stops, the hours log stops with it because the habit was bundled, the crisis shift is dropped "until things settle," and in November 2029 there is no entry in any ledger and no way to tell when it stopped.

That year is recoverable if the hours log survived it and unrecoverable if it did not.
