The Wednesday, in order

  1. First
    Logs and calendar. Five minutes. Anything from the week not yet recorded, then the next dated thing above.
  2. Two hours
    The review. Screening, extraction or synthesis. It needs three years to reach print, so it gets the best hours of the day rather than what is left of them.
  3. One hour
    The notebook. Reading notes, review progress, or a practitioner conversation written up. Monthly is the floor; weekly is this season.
  4. Then
    One ladder action. An email, a registration, a course week. Small, and always moving.
  5. Fortnightly
    The spoken piece. One take, trimmed but not edited. Rehearsal for a mock session recording in 2033 and an interview in 2031.

And on the longer beats

  1. Monthly
    Last Wednesday. One resource documented and licensed. The boundary pages skimmed. One name in the relational ledger — one contact, one record.
  2. Twice a year
    April and October. Two faculty profiles reread, one recent paper read, the reading note written into the notebook. One practitioner interview.
  3. Annually
    First Wednesday of November. The five ledgers reviewed, the verification list struck through, and the question rewritten — one paragraph, dated, appended, never overwritten.

The floor, in every season: one notebook entry a month, the hours log within twenty-four hours of any service, the crisis-line shift kept, and one calendar check a month. Everything else is allowed to stop.
