# House rules — the pre-licence boundary

*Working note, 20 September 2026. Consolidated from `therapeutic-studio-the-approach.md`, which remains the source; this is the operational copy kept beside the work. These are the rules that let the public side of Therapeutic Studio stay public.*

**They are stricter than the law currently requires, because the law changes on 29 November 2027**, when psychotherapy becomes a regulated profession under the College of Health and Care Professionals of BC. Everything published before then should already read as if that date had passed. Verified: HPOA in force 1 April 2026; psychotherapy regulated 29 November 2027; college applications open summer 2027.

---

## Words

- **Use:** *facilitator*, *arts-based facilitator*, *creative health practitioner*, *peer*.
- **Never:** *counsellor*, *therapist*, *psychotherapist*, *clinician* — or any phrasing that implies one: *sessions*, *clients*, *treatment*, *intervention for depression* offered as a service.
- Make Space is *a creative health practice* and *a public inquiry*. It is **not therapy**, and the page says so once, plainly, without apology.
- "Therapeutic" in the studio's name describes the register of the work, not a service. The welcome page carries the one-sentence version of that.

> **Open item.** BC is regulating *psychotherapy*, and the reserved title has not yet been prescribed — CCPA and BCACC jointly proposed "Clinical Counsellor" or "Psychotherapist" in 2023 and the Ministry has not chosen. **Verify whether a property named *Therapeutic* Studio attracts advertising-standards attention once the title is protected.** Not a reason to reopen the naming decision settled in `06-the-naming-and-the-room.md`; a reason to have the answer before November 2027. Tracked in `verification.md`, item 10.

## Acts

- No one-to-one work framed as help with a mental health problem. Group, peer and open sessions only.
- No assessment, diagnosis or clinical advice — in the room, in the community, in comments, or by direct message. Keep a standing reply, with the Crisis Centre BC line and 9-8-8.
- **No screening measures, outcome data, participant research, pre/post measures or instrument development before the MA.** Anything needing a consent sheet waits for a supervisor and a research ethics board.
- **Referral, not retention.** If someone in the room needs more than the room, the job is to say so kindly and name where to go.

## Records

Two ledgers, never mixed.

| | Holds | Lives in |
|---|---|---|
| **Public** | The Substack, the YouTube channel, the notebook, the tools, the resource library | `therapeutic-practice` (this repo) and the public sites |
| **Private** | The reflective journal, incident notes, the hours log, references | `therapeutic-practice-private` — a **separate repository**, never this one |

- Anything with a person in it is **anonymised at the point of writing, not later**.
- The private ledger is what the master's application and later papers draw on. The public one is what a supervisor reads first.
- See `README.md` → *The boundary* for why these are two repositories rather than one repository with a `.gitignore`.

## Respect for the licensed

- Cite and credit registered practitioners and their frameworks by name when the work draws on them.
- **Interview an RCC, RCAT or psychologist for the notebook twice a year** — visible, collegial, credited. One of them becomes a standing reader by 2027 and a reference by 2029.
- Read the BCACC Code of Ethics and the CATA standards now, and hold the studio to them voluntarily. Note this on the about page in one line.
- **When CHCPBC publishes its standards, re-read every public page against them.** Diarised for 29 November 2027 in `calendar-data.js`.

## Never from your own workplace

Carried from `therapeutic-studio-the-question.md` and it becomes sharper as the plan works: peer-programme volunteering and lab work at the institution that employs you need the employer's written knowledge, and the institution's outside-activities and conflict-of-interest policy read first. **Students who are or could be your advisees are not peers you support.**

This is the same clause that `the-studio-day-pattern.md` flags as blocking for Accessible Learning Labs. It is one contract and it governs three properties. Read it once. Tracked in `verification.md`, item 12.

## The application boundary

This system prepares the record, the training plan, the calendar and the evidence.

**It does not draft the statement of interest, the reference requests in your voice, or the video answers.** Those are yours alone, as the department requires. No AI touches `application-2030/` — the rule is written into that folder's own README and into `guard.sh`.

## The test

If a draft blurs the peer/clinical line, that gets said before anything else — before the writing is discussed, before the idea is assessed. The line is the condition of the work, not a feature of it.
