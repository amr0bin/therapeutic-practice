# Incidents

*Private. Started 20 September 2026. Separate from the reflective journal on
purpose: reflection is for learning, this is for record.*

**What goes here.** Anything that went wrong, was escalated, was reported, needed
a supervisor in the moment, involved a risk decision, or that you would want a
contemporaneous dated note of if it were raised a year later.

**Why keep it before there is any clinical work.** Because the crisis line starts
in 2027, and because a contemporaneous note written on the day is worth something
that a recollection written in 2031 is not. The habit is the point, and it is the
same habit a regulator expects from 29 November 2027.

**Anonymised at the point of writing.** Same rule as the journal, and it is
harder here, because incidents have specifics. Record what *you* did and what the
service did. Not who it was.

**The service's own record governs.** Crisis Centre BC, a lab, a placement or an
employer each have their own incident process, and theirs is the official record.
This file never replaces it, never contradicts it, and never holds anything the
organisation's process says should stay with the organisation. It is your own
dated note that you followed the process.

---

## If this file is ever empty for a long stretch of active service

That is worth noticing at the November review. It usually means one of two
things, and they are opposite: either the work is genuinely quiet, or the
threshold for "worth recording" has drifted upward — which is the earlier warning
sign of the two.
