# References and the relational ledger

*Private. Started 20 September 2026. The public half of this is `faculty-map.md`,
which holds published research interests and nothing else. Who replied, what was
said and what was asked for lives here.*

**Why dates matter more than names.** `the-ledgers.md` makes the relational ledger
the mechanism by which the practicum seat opens, and notes that it is the only
ledger that cannot be produced in the year it is needed. A referee asked six
months before a deadline writes a different letter from one asked in November.

**Annual rate:** two new named relationships, each kept warm with two contacts a
year. Reviewed at the November review.
