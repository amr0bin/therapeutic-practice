# application-2030

**This folder is yours alone.**

UBC states that no AI may be used on the application itself.
`therapeutic-studio-the-approach.md` turns that into a house rule, and
`house-rules.md` restates it as the application boundary:

> This system prepares the record, the training plan, the calendar and the
> evidence. It does not draft the statement of interest, the reference requests
> in your voice, or the video answers.

`guard.sh` in the public repository refuses any commit touching this path. The
honest version of the rule is that there is nothing to enforce, because you do
not ask.

---

## What lives here, from 2029

- The statement of interest, drafted and redrafted
- The written answers to any programme-specific questions
- The video script or notes, if a video assessment turns out to be real
  (**unverified** at UBC — see `verification.md`, item 5)
- The reference request messages
- The submitted versions, archived per institution and per year

## What it is written from

Not from a blank page in November 2030.

- **`the-question.md`** — by then, four or five dated paragraphs on what the
  practice has found. The statement is assembled from a trajectory rather than
  reconstructed under deadline. This is the single reason that file exists.
- **`faculty-map.md`** — why *this* department, named, with a person in it who
  has read the review.
- **The published review** — the evidence base, already citable.
- **`hours-log.csv`** — the three years of helping experience, with dates.

## The rehearsal

The fortnightly spoken piece, running since 2026. Seven years of saying it aloud
before anyone assesses it. Its verified target is the 20–30 minute mock
counselling session recording required for the **practicum** application in
January 2033 — a different and later thing than admissions, and a real one.
