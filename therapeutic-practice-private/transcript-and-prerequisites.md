## Why the grades matter, not only the passes

Both institutions assess a senior average: UBC wants **B+ / 76% in third- and
fourth-year courses**; SFU wants **3.0 cumulative or 3.33 over the last 60 units**.

A prerequisite taken carelessly is a third- or fourth-year course that pulls the
average that admits you. These are not boxes to tick, and a course taken in a
crowded term is worse than a course taken a term later.

## Funding

Both universities' staff waivers cover undergraduate credits for essentially all
staff groups — UBC at 12 credits per 12-month period after three months' service;
SFU under policy AD 9.10. **The prerequisites are undergraduate courses**, so a
staff post at either institution pays for this ladder entirely.

SFU's waiver has a submission window worth diarising: no earlier than week 6 of
the previous term, no later than the tuition deadline in week 2, with 2% monthly
interest on late amounts.
