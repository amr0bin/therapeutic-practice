# The faculty map

*Working note, 20 September 2026. The counselling equivalent of Studio Day's `atlas.html` — the people the practice is in conversation with, and the ones who can open a door. First pass, from published research interests verified 20 September 2026.*

**Why this is started now rather than in 2029.** `therapeutic-studio-the-approach.md` defers this to 2029. That is too late. Faculty fit is built by being in someone's field for years — citing them, attending what they run, asking one good question at a conference, sending the preprint — not by discovering them in a final autumn. A supervisor who has already read the notebook does not need a statement of interest to know the fit, and `therapeutic-studio-the-question.md` names that as the whole point of Track 2.

**What this file is not.** Nothing here is a note about a relationship. Research interests are public; who replied, what was said, and what was asked for are private and live in `references.md` in the private repository. **This file stays public and impersonal.**

## Two gaps, and they are findings rather than absences

**No SFU counselling psychology faculty publishes depression as a named research focus.** Prevention appears once, via Cameron. Measurement appears as *assessment practice* rather than psychometrics.

**Nobody at either institution publishes arts-based methods within counselling psychology.** Bai's "aesthetic dimensions of education" is adjacent and is philosophy of education.

So: **the creative-health angle will be argued into the programme, not found waiting there.** That is much better known in 2026 than in 2030. Three consequences:

1. The thesis proposal should lead with the **access and stepped-care** question, which has supervisors at both institutions, and carry creative health as the intervention being examined rather than as the frame.
2. The **convergence move** in `05-the-two-doctoral-routes.md` — a thesis legible at both the counselling door and the practice-based art door — is harder than that note assumes on the counselling side. It is not impossible; it needs Hubley's measurement or Jordan's evaluation as the method, with creative health as the object.
3. If the arts-based frame is non-negotiable, that is an argument for the **art-side doctoral route**, and it is information the 2032 decision should carry.

## What the depth chart actually says

Reading the two institutions as a whole rather than name by name:

- **UBC is where depression and measurement live**, and it is the only counselling psychology doctoral door in BC.
- **SFU is where community-engaged evaluation and disability-as-student-experience live**, and where the counselling service already publishes the pathway this practice is aimed at.

That is a real tension and it will not resolve itself. It resolves when one named person wants the question.

## How this file is worked

**Twice a year, at the monthly Wednesday in April and October:**

- Re-read two profiles. Interests change; grant cycles change who is taking students.
- Check "accepting students" status for anyone on the shortlist.
- Read one recent paper by one person on this list and write the reading note into the notebook. That is the ledger entry and the preparation in the same action.

**Annually, at the November review:** revise the shortlist to three names per institution, and record in `references.md` (private) who has been contacted and when.

**From 2029:** the supervisor conversation. Not a cold email — a message from someone who has cited them, read them, and has a preprint to send.
