# Registration

*Working note, 20 September 2026. What has to be true to practise in British Columbia, and — more usefully — what has to be chosen **inside the master's, in 2032**, for it to be true in 2035. Verified 20 September 2026; sourced in `verification.md`.*

**Why this file exists early:** the registration requirements are met by decisions made years before anyone reads them, and two of them are elective course choices made for reasons that have nothing to do with registration. This file is here so that in 2032 the choice is already written down.

## The landscape as of today

| | Status |
|---|---|
| **HPOA** (Health Professions and Occupations Act) | In force **1 April 2026** |
| **Counselling therapists regulated** | **29 November 2027** — psychotherapy becomes CHCPBC's 10th regulated profession |
| **College applications open** | Summer 2027 |
| **Bylaw consultation** | Autumn 2026. **Webinar 14 October 2026** |
| **Entry-to-practice standards** | **Not yet established.** CHCPBC states this explicitly |
| **The reserved title** | **Not yet prescribed.** CCPA and BCACC jointly proposed "Clinical Counsellor" or "Psychotherapist" in 2023; the Ministry has not chosen |
| **Grandparenting** | *"No designations will be automatically ported."* Two routes expected — legacy, and regular for recent graduates. Criteria unpublished |

**What this means for someone graduating around 2034:** registration will be with **CHCPBC under the new regime**, via the *regular route for recent graduates*, not via BCACC's RCC as the primary licence, and not via the legacy route (which is for people already practising at transition).

**The requirements for that route do not exist yet.** Anyone who tells you otherwise is speculating.

## The hedge, and it is the whole strategy

Since CHCPBC's bar cannot be targeted, **clear the most demanding published standard that currently exists.** That means satisfying BCACC's RCC *and* CCPA's CCC simultaneously, because they measure different things and a programme that clears both has cleared more ground than either alone.

### RCC — BCACC

A master's in clinical counselling, psychotherapy or a relevant preparatory area, covering **six core topic areas**:

| | Area | Usually covered by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Developmental psychology across the lifespan **or** psychopathology | Core coursework |
| 2 | Counselling theories **or** personality theories | Core coursework |
| 3 | **Group therapy theory and practice** | **An elective. This is the trap** |
| 4 | **Family therapy theory and practice** | **An elective. This is the other trap** |
| 5 | Basic research design | Core, and the MA thesis route covers it comfortably |
| 6 | Professional ethics in counselling/psychotherapy | Core — UBC has CNPS 586 |

Plus **at least 100 hours of clinical supervision, of which at least 25 must be direct**. Two references via BCACC's own forms. Canadian residency. $200 application fee. No exam is mentioned anywhere in the eligibility page or the Applications Guidebook.

**And the item that surprises people:** BCACC's Guidebook states *"BCACC does not require you to report client contact hours, only clinical supervision hours."* There is **no published direct-client-contact threshold for RCC.**

### CCC — CCPA

The mirror image. **Eight standalone graduate courses**: counselling theories; counselling and communication skills; supervised counselling practicum; professional ethics; plus **four electives** from CCPA's designated areas, maximum two per category.

Hours: **minimum 150 direct client counselling hours** in supervised practicum, with at least 75% meeting "relational processing" standards. **Minimum 10 hours of direct supervision.** No exam.

### The comparison that matters

| | RCC | CCC |
|---|---|---|
| Direct client hours | **None required** | **150** |
| Supervision hours | **100, with 25 direct** | **10 direct** |
| Course structure | Six topic areas | Eight named courses |

They are not interchangeable. **Plan to the higher of each: 150+ direct client hours and 100+ supervision hours with 25+ direct.** A UBC Counselling Services practicum alone delivers 180 direct hours and roughly 100+ supervision hours across the year, which clears both — which is another reason the practicum seat in `the-ceiling.md` is the hinge rather than a formality.

## The two decisions to make in 2032, written down in 2026

**Take group therapy theory and practice. Take family therapy theory and practice.**

These are the two areas people most often fail to have on the transcript when they apply for RCC, because they are elective choices made in the second year of a programme for reasons of interest, timetable or supervisor — none of which are registration reasons. A transcript missing either one means additional coursework after graduation, at your own cost, while not yet registered and not yet earning.

Group therapy has a second argument behind it: **both universities' counselling services run group programmes** — UBC lists "wellness programming facilitation" as a duty in the Wellness Advisor posting, and SFU runs groups from single-session to eight weeks and states they "can be even more effective than individual counselling." Group facilitation is directly employable, immediately, and it is on the registration list anyway.

**Also worth checking in 2031, on arrival:** that the programme's ethics course, research design course and practicum are each the ones that will be named on the registration application, and that the practicum's supervision hours are logged in a form a registrar accepts. `supervision-log.csv` in the private repository exists for this.

## The honest uncertainty

Four things could move, and the plan should survive each:

- **Entry-to-practice standards are unwritten.** A programme satisfying RCC today may or may not satisfy CHCPBC in 2035.
- **Whether BCACC membership still matters after regulation is unresolved.** CCPA argues associations retain an advocacy role; that is an association's position, not a regulator's statement.
- **Timelines have already moved once** — designation in July 2024, regulation in November 2027. No published contingency exists if November 2027 slips.
- **CCC's trademark stays with CCPA** regardless of regulation, and holding CCC does not guarantee college licensure.

None of these are reasons to wait. They are reasons to clear the highest published bar, keep the logs in a registrar-legible format from the first entry, and be in the consultation room this October.
