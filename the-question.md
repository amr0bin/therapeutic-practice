# The question

*One paragraph, rewritten each November and dated. Never overwritten: each year is added below the one before it, so the version history and this file both hold the trajectory. Mirrors `question.md` in the Studio Day repository, which does the same job for the painting.*

---

**The governing question**, carried since 11 September 2026 and governed by `therapeutic-studio-the-question.md`:

> **Who does campus stepped care for depression leave out — disabled students first among them — and what low-intensity, accessible provision would reach them without displacing the licensed room?**

That sentence is the practice's fixed point. What follows is not a restatement of it. It is what the year found out about it.

---

**What each entry answers:** what the practice has found this year. Not what was done, and not what the work is about.

**Why it exists:** every application, every supervisor conversation, every reference request and every doctoral file asks a version of this. It costs nothing, and eight dated versions of it are evidence of sustained independent inquiry that no coursework can produce. The 2030 statement of interest is written from this file rather than reconstructed under deadline.

**How to write it:** one paragraph, under two hundred words, in the register of the notebook rather than in application language. Write it on the first Wednesday of November, after the five ledgers are reviewed, so it is written against a year of actual work rather than against an intention.

**One rule specific to this practice:** nothing client-derived, ever, at any stage, de-identified or not. The question is answered from the literature, from the room in general terms, and from your own thinking. Not from anyone who came to you.
