## Open — ranked by what they cost to get wrong

### 1 · The UBC department pages have never been read
`ecps.educ.ubc.ca` sits behind a UBC Cybersecurity JavaScript challenge and could not be reached programmatically. **Every UBC fact in this repository comes from the Academic Calendar and Graduate & Postdoctoral Studies**, which are authoritative on structure and may lag departmental practice on admissions detail.

**Action:** open the department site in an ordinary browser and read the admissions pages, the faculty roster and the practicum guide. One sitting. It resolves items 2–5 and 8 below.

### 2 · Is the GRE still required for UBC's MA?
**Highest priority.** UBC's Graduate Studies MA page states *"The GRE is required by all applicants"*; the Academic Calendar agrees; the MEd and PhD pages both say not required. Many counselling programmes dropped the GRE after 2020 without updating central pages, and the department page is where a rescission would appear first.

It lands on the **MA (thesis)** route — the one already committed to for doctoral reasons — and costs money and months in 2029. **Until answered: assume required and budget for it.**

### 3 · The prerequisite equivalency tables, both institutions
UBC publishes *"Approved M.A. & M.Ed. Prerequisites"* — confirmed to exist, contents unread. SFU publishes no equivalency table found.

**The specific question:** can UBC's CNPS 362/365 and SFU's counselling-skills/counselling-theories requirements be satisfied by the same two courses? Best case six courses, worst case nine. Three terms of difference. See `prerequisites.md`.

### 4 · Does UBC adjudicate prerequisites before application?
`therapeutic-studio-the-approach.md` states pre-application adjudication is no longer offered. Unconfirmed from any accessible page.

### 5 · Is there a video assessment in UBC admissions, and an AI-use policy?
`therapeutic-studio-the-approach.md` states both as fact. Neither could be verified. **SFU's interview is confirmed.** What *is* confirmed is a 20–30 minute mock counselling session recording required for the **practicum** application at UBC, and 25–30 minutes at SFU — a different thing, two years later. Downgraded from fact to open.

### 6 · How does SFU grant MA faculty approval, and when?
SFU's page: students *"Complete an MEd or an MA (with faculty approval)."* Neither elective nor a separate admission stream — a gate passed after entry. **If SFU is chosen and the thesis matters, this is a Year 1 problem and the answer changes whether SFU is viable at all.**

### 7 · Employment contract and outside-activities clause
Carried from `the-studio-day-pattern.md`, where it is marked **blocking**. It governs three properties, not one: lab volunteering and peer-programme work at an employing institution as much as Labs resources and this repository.

**Read it before entering a lab, joining a campus peer programme, or publishing a resource.**

### 8 · Total practicum and direct-contact hours for both master's programmes
UBC publishes credits, not hours; the ECPS Practicum Placement Guide v9.2 was unreachable. SFU publishes no total hour figure. **Do not infer hours from credits.** One external site's standard (UBC Counselling Services: 600 total, 180 direct) is not the programme requirement.

### 9 · Did UBC's CACEP accreditation renew past 30 September 2026?
Listed expiry 30 September 2026 for both MA and MEd; re-application filed 28 August 2025. Presumably routine. Confirm on the CCPA accreditation page after that date.

### 10 · Does a property named *Therapeutic* Studio raise advertising-standards questions once the title is protected?
BC is regulating *psychotherapy*; the reserved title is not yet prescribed. Advertising standards follow the registrant everywhere. **Not a reason to reopen the naming settled in `06-the-naming-and-the-room.md` — a reason to have the answer before 29 November 2027.**

### 11 · CHCPBC entry-to-practice standards for the regular route
**Do not exist yet.** CHCPBC states so explicitly. The hedge is in `registration.md`: clear the highest published bar — RCC's six areas and 100/25 supervision, plus CCC's 150 direct hours. Revisit after the autumn 2026 bylaw consultation and again when standards publish.

### 12 · The prevalence claim about disabled students and depression
Stated from memory in `my-vision.html`, consistent with the literature, **still uncited**. It blocks every public statement of the governing question. One afternoon's work, and it has been outstanding since 11 September 2026.

### 13 · Smaller, and worth knowing
- Whether MA/MEd transfer after admission is possible at UBC.
- Any CNPS-specific GPA minimum above the Faculty's 76% floor.
- Whether MERM faculty (Zumbo, Kroc, Liao) supervise across ECPS programmes.
- Whether SFU counsellors sit in APSA, and whether either university gives internal candidates priority in hiring.
- Whether the SFU PhD in Education formally requires a thesis-based master's — only a general steer was found.

- Whether 1-800-SUICIDE still operates as a distinct line, and which lines Crisis Centre volunteers staff.
