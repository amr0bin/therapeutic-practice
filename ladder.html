The slow accumulator—

The ladder, and the counted things

Cheap, slow, unglamorous, and the thing that closes the door by inaction rather than by rejection. Six courses if the two institutions' equivalency tables agree with each other; nine if they do not. That difference is three terms, and it is currently unknown.

The prerequisite courses

    Hard boundary: UBC requires every prerequisite complete by 31 December of the application year — 31 December 2030. At one course a term from the inventory this is comfortable. At two a term it competes with the crisis-line shift, and the shift is the three-year clock.

    Fund it. These are undergraduate courses, and both universities' staff tuition waivers cover undergraduate credits for essentially all staff groups. A staff post at either institution pays for this ladder entirely.

    Totals only — no dates, no settings, no people, so this page can stay public. Updated from the private logs at the monthly Wednesday.
