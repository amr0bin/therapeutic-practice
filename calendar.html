Somebody else's dates

External deadlines, on somebody else's schedule. Self-imposed deadlines are not deadlines and are not in this file. Everything here carries a status: verified means the date was read on the organisation's own page; expected means it is projected from the published cycle and must be confirmed when the window opens.

    Reconnaissance is free. Both application portals open and close every year, whether or not it is your year. the-studio-day-pattern.md makes the same point about job postings: watching the cycle while nothing is at stake costs nothing and tells you exactly what the file will need.
