# The prerequisite ladder

*Working note, 20 September 2026. The slow accumulator. Cheap, unglamorous, and the thing that closes the door by inaction rather than by rejection. Lists verified 20 September 2026 and sourced in `verification.md`.*

**Why this file exists:** `the-studio-day-pattern.md` calls this "the highest-value action available in this property and close to the cheapest," and `therapeutic-studio-the-approach.md` says the inventory can wait until 2029. With SFU's list now on the table that is no longer true — the two universities want **different** courses, and the union is roughly eight rather than five. Eight courses at one a term is four years. The application is in December 2030. The slack is smaller than it looks.

**Hard boundary:** UBC requires all prerequisites complete by **31 December of the application year** — 31 December 2030.

## How the ladder is climbed

**One course a term, starting as soon as the inventory is done.** Not two. Two courses a term against a full-time role and Leeds is how the crisis-line shift gets dropped, and the shift is the three-year clock.

**Fund it.** The prerequisites are undergraduate courses, and both universities' staff tuition waivers cover undergraduate credits for essentially all staff groups — UBC at 12 credits per 12-month period, SFU under AD 9.10. A staff post at either institution pays for this ladder entirely. That is one of the strongest practical arguments for ring one in `08-the-target-map.md`, and it is why `the-ceiling.md` lists it under asymmetric moves.

**Place them in the module breaks.** During Leeds (Sept 2027 – Aug 2029) a course fits in a break far better than it fits in a term.

**Record the grades.** The B+ / 76% senior average is assessed on third- and fourth-year courses, so a prerequisite taken carelessly can pull the average that admits you. These are not boxes to tick.
